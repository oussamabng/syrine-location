Add original car photographs in a subfolder named after the vehicle slug:

  public/cars/gallery/geely-coolray-2026-gris/01.webp
  public/cars/gallery/geely-coolray-2026-gris/02.jpg

The website automatically discovers .webp, .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .avif files.
They appear after the original catalogue photo in numeric file-name order.
No changes to data.ts or the gallery component are needed.
To ensure all photos appear with a production build, rebuild after adding files.
Only add photos you have permission to use; other vehicles' photos should never
be presented as pictures of a specific Syrine rental car.
